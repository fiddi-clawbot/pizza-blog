# Franco Pepe

**Centro Storico, Perugia**

Franco Pepe isn't just a pizzeria—it's a philosophy about food, region, and craft. Located in Perugia's old town, the restaurant champions Umbrian ingredients and traditional techniques with quiet confidence.

The space feels lived-in. Wooden tables, warm light, the smell of wood smoke and good food. This is where locals eat, not tourists seeking Instagram moments.

**The Approach**

Pepe sources directly from regional producers. The flour comes from a specific mill. The tomatoes are from specific farms. The olive oil is Umbrian. This isn't performative localism—it's how he learned to cook.

Pizza here is part of a larger meal. You might start with crescia (a traditional bread), move to pizza, and finish with pasta. Each dish builds on the same philosophy: respect ingredients, technique, tradition.

**Signature Pizzas**

- **Tartufo**: Black truffle (seasonal), mozzarella, cream.
- **Mortadella & Pecorino**: The best mortadella you've had, paired with aged pecorino.
- **Simple Pomodoro**: Let the tomato speak.

**Why It Matters**

Franco Pepe represents an alternative to the Rome-Naples pizza narrative. Regional Italian pizza traditions matter. The food of Umbria, Tuscany, and beyond deserves the same respect Neapolitan pizza receives. He's proof.
