# Al Taglio

**Prati, Rome**

Roman pizza *al taglio* (by the cut) at its finest. Gabriele Bonci's flagship pizzeria transformed how people think about Roman-style pizza. The focus: exceptional ingredients, minimal processing, maximum flavor.

Walk in and the visual is overwhelming—enormous trays of pizza in every direction. The colors are natural: greens from fresh vegetables, whites from quality cheese, golden-browns from proper fermentation.

**The Philosophy**

Roman pizza is different from Neapolitan. Higher hydration, longer fermentation (24-72 hours), thinner and crispier. Bonci took this tradition and elevated it by sourcing ingredients obsessively and treating each topping as part of the whole composition.

The dough is the star. Everything else serves it.

**What to Order**

- **Romana**: Simple tomato, basil, mozzarella. Pure.
- **Zucchini & Pecorino**: Sweet courgette, aged pecorino, simple olive oil.
- **Anchovy & Tomato**: Intense, balanced, traditional.

Pick 2-3 slices. The experience is in variety, not volume.

**Why It Matters**

Al Taglio made Roman pizza aspirational. It proved that working within tradition—not against it—could produce something revolutionary. Every pizzeria doing "elevated Roman pizza" today owes something to Bonci's influence.
